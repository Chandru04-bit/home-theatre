Since 2011, AVÉRA Cinema has united physics, architectural elegance, and cutting-edge cinema technology for the world's most discerning homeowners.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Born from a Passion for Master-Tape Authenticity

We founded AVÉRA Cinema on a simple conviction: commercial movie theatres compromise sound for crowd volume, while typical residential installers prioritize hardware margins over room acoustic physics.

A truly transcendent home theatre doesn't begin in a cardboard box of speakers—it begins with room geometry, modal resonance decay calculations, and structural sound isolation. Over the past 15 years, our CEDIA, ISF, and THX-certified team has engineered over 280 bespoke screening rooms across Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Hyderabad.

ENGINEERING CREED

The Four AVÉRA Principles

Every blueprint, cable termination, and calibration curve reflects our unwavering commitment to precision.

Acoustic Science First

No hardware can fix standing wave cancellations or flutter echoes. We model room RT60 decay and structural decoupling before a single wire is pulled.

Architectural Purity

Technology must serve architecture. Zero-bezel in-wall speakers, concealed motorized drop screens, and silent rack ventilation keep spaces visually pristine.

Surgical Calibration

We calibrate every seat using Dirac Live multi-seat impulse correction, multi-subwoofer phase synchronization, and ISF Level-III spectrophotometers.

Lifelong Client Care

Our relationship only begins on premiere night. All private cinemas include 5-year craftsmanship guarantees, annual tune-ups, and priority concierge hotlines.
