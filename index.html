ENGINEERING PROCESS

The Path to Perfection

Our systematic 4-phase commissioning process ensures flawless acoustic fidelity, architectural elegance, and effortless operation.

01

Consultation

We assess your visual and audio aspirations, seating layout preferences, and performance expectations.

02

Room Assessment

Laser measurement, structural resonance analysis, ambient light evaluation, and electrical profiling.

03

System Design

04
