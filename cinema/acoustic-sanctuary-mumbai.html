PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

Auditorium Splendor in a Private Seaside Residence

Located on the lower ground level of a bespoke sea-facing estate in Bandra West, Mumbai, this private screening room is built to match the output and accuracy of Hollywood grading theatres while providing the ultimate in client luxury.

Floated Kinetic Isolation Floor

The entire 16-seat auditorium rests on a decoupled floating concrete slab isolated by high-deflection spring mounts. This prevents mechanical vibrations from low-frequency bass notes down to 14Hz from transferring upwards into the main residence.

Quad-Subwoofer Phase Alignment

Four 18-inch subwoofers placed symmetrically along room room nodes are individually powered and processed through Dirac Live Active Room Treatment, creating an extraordinarily flat, articulate bass response from front row to back row.

ENGINEERED METRICS

Technical Specifications

Every acoustic reflection, speaker angle, and riser height was precisely modeled in 3D acoustic simulation.

Audio System

9.4.4 Dolby Atmos / DTS:X Pro

Wisdom Audio planar magnetic line-source speakers, StormAudio 24-channel ISP Elite processor, and four 18-inch custom subwoofers.

Screen & Optics

Barco Balder 4K Laser

Barco Residential Cinemascope 4K Laser projector beaming onto a 200-inch curved acoustically transparent Seymour-Screen Excellence canvas.

Seating Capacity

Sixteen custom motorized zero-gravity recliners in heated perforated Italian nappa leather arranged across three curved acoustic tiers.

Acoustic Engineering

Floating Room-in-Room
