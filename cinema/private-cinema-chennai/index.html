PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

Bespoke Residential Screening Auditorium

Situated within a private estate on Boat Club Road, Chennai, this private screening room was engineered from the studs up to satisfy the rigorous technical demands of professional film editors while honoring the refined architectural aesthetic of a luxury residence.

Acoustic Decoupling & Isolation

The room incorporates a full box-in-box construction: a floating reinforced concrete slab resting on high-deflection elastomeric isolators, decoupled double-stud drywall assemblies, and an engineered isolated ceiling grid. The acoustic envelope maintains an NC-18 background noise rating, ensuring complete silence even during intense ambient rainstorms.

Precision Audio Calibration

The 7.2.4 channel configuration employs custom-angled Triad architectural in-wall loudspeakers driven by high-current monoblock amplification. Multiple active subwoofers are phase-synchronized using Dirac Live Active Room Treatment to eliminate standing bass waves, resulting in uniform, tight punch at every seated position.

ENGINEERED METRICS

Technical Specifications

Every transducer, acoustic baffle, and optical throw angle was calculated to reference cinema exhibition standards.

Sound System Details

7.2.4 Dolby Atmos

Discrete 13-channel spatial immersion. Triad in-wall architectural drivers, dual sealed 15-inch high-excursion subwoofers, StormAudio 16-channel processor, and Dirac Live Room Correction.

Screen & Optics

Sony Native 4K Laser

Sony VPL-XW7000ES Native 4K SXRD Laser Projector beaming onto a 165-inch 2.35:1 Anamorphic Acoustically Transparent Micro-Perforated Stewart Filmscreen with motorized masking.

Seating Capacity

9 Tiered Recliners

Nine motorized Cineak Fortuny Italian top-grain leather cinema recliners arranged across two tiered acoustic risers with concealed LED step lighting, heating, and articulating headrests.

Acoustic Treatment

Decoupled Box-In-Box

Decoupled walls with Green Glue viscoelastic damping, concealed high-absorption velvet panels, rear quadratic residue diffusers (QRD), and tuned membrane bass traps.

Lighting & Control

Control4 & Fiber Optics

Starlit fiber-optic constellation ceiling with shooting stars, Lutron motorized architectural blackout drapery, and single-touch "Play Movie" automation via Control4 Neeo remote.

Location & Certification

Boat Club Road, Chennai

Turnkey residential design, 3D CAD acoustic modeling, equipment rack ventilation engineering, and full CEDIA RP22 performance certification completed in 8 weeks.
