PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

Cinema-Grade Visuals in an Open Sunlit Penthouse

Overlooking the vibrant Bengaluru skyline, this penthouse living suite presented a unique challenge: delivering true cinematic contrast and immersion in an open-concept living space flooded with natural light from floor-to-ceiling glass balconies.

Ambient Light Rejection Optics

We integrated a 120-inch motorized Ambient Light Rejecting (ALR) Fresnel optical screen paired with an ultra-short-throw triple laser projector. The multi-layered lenticular structure selectively absorbs 93% of ceiling and window illumination while reflecting laser photons directly to the viewers, producing deep inky blacks even in broad daylight.

Architectural Timber Acoustics

The media wall features custom-fabricated acoustic walnut timber slats backed by sound-absorbing recycled PET felt, controlling room reverberation without clashing with the modern Scandinavian interior. Flush in-wall speakers disappear behind paintable magnetic grilles.

ENGINEERED METRICS

Technical Specifications

Engineered for unobtrusive luxury, invisible technology, and high-performance daily entertainment.

Sound System Details

5.1.2 Spatial Audio

Architectural Kevlar-cone in-wall front channels, dual angled ceiling Atmos speakers, and a hidden active subwoofer calibrated via Anthem ARC Genesis.

Screen & Optics

4K Laser UST + 120" ALR

Formovie Theater RGB Triple-Laser 4K projector delivering 2,800 ANSI Lumens, 3000:1 native contrast, and Dolby Vision on a 120-inch optical ALR fixed frame screen.

Lounge Seating

Curved Bouclé Modular

Custom Italian curved modular sectional upholstered in stain-resistant ivory bouclé fabric, oriented for conversational socializing and optimal viewing angles.

Acoustic Treatment

Timber Slat Absorption

Full-height vertical acoustic oak slats with rear acoustic felt absorbing mid-high frequency reflections from panoramic window glass and polished stone flooring.

Lighting & Shading

Lutron Palladiom Shades

Dual-roller Lutron automated shading system combining sheer daylight diffusing screens with motorized blackout roller shades for instant daytime cinema transition.
