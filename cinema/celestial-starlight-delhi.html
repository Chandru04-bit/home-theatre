PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

Grand Cinematic Wonder Under a Living Night Sky

Commissioned for an expansive luxury estate in Golf Links, New Delhi, this 12-seat screening auditorium transports viewers beneath a nocturnal sky while delivering reference-grade IMAX Enhanced and Dolby Atmos acoustics.

Bespoke Shooting-Star Constellation

The multi-tiered ceiling incorporates over 1,200 fiber-optic strands with variable diameters to simulate true astronomical star magnitudes, programmed with algorithmic twinkling and periodic shooting stars that sweep gracefully across the vaulted ceiling.

Motorized Velvet Scope Masking

A 180-inch 2.35:1 curved Cinemascope screen is framed by automated deep navy velvet masking drapes that automatically adjust their aspect ratio to eliminate black letterbox bars whether playing Hollywood wide releases or 16:9 sporting events.

ENGINEERED METRICS

Technical Specifications

Engineered for unforgettable family entertainment, effortless automation, and reference optical dynamics.

Audio System

9.2.4 Dolby Atmos

Marantz Cinema amplification, bi-amplified high-SPL surround channels, dual 15-inch active sealed subwoofers, and Dirac Live calibration.

Screen & Optics

Sony SXRD + 180" Scope

Sony Native 4K SXRD Laser Projector with anamorphic motorization on a 180-inch curved micro-perforated Stewart Filmscreen.

Seating Capacity

12 Motorized Recliners

Twelve dual-motor luxury recliners with USB-C charging, integrated cup warmers/chillers, and soft step-lighting on decoupled risers.

Acoustics

Fabric Stretch Acoustic Walls

Architectural track-and-stretch acoustic velvet wall fabric concealing high-density mineral wool absorption panels and bass traps.

Automation & Lighting

Control4 Automation

Single-button scene activation: lights slowly fade down, curtains part, projector powers on, and fiber-optic stars begin their celestial show.
