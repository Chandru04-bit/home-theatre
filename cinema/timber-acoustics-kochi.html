PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

Warm Kerala Teak Harmonics with Studio Isolation

Nestled within a premier villa in Panampilly Nagar, Kochi, this screening room marries authentic hand-rubbed Kerala teak woodwork with modern European acoustical engineering, concealing reference cinema transducers completely behind architectural finishes.

Vertical Teak Resonance Management

Each timber slat is machined from mature plantation teak with variable spacing to disrupt specular flutter echoes. Concealed behind the slats are tuned high-density acoustic membranes that absorb modal bass room reflections without muffling dialogue frequencies.

Micro-Perforated Celestial Canopy

The dark charcoal acoustic ceiling combines micro-perforated acoustic baffling with an 800-fiber optical constellation array, providing exceptional overhead sound absorption while creating a magical starlit night sky above the viewers.
