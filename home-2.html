FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Private Cinema Architecture FAQs

Essential insights into room sizing, acoustic decoupling, and smart integration.

We design dedicated private cinemas starting from 180 sq. ft. (12' x 15') up to 1,500+ sq. ft. estate screening auditoriums. Our acoustic team conducts 3D EASE ray-tracing to calculate custom speaker angles, bass trap depths, and sightlines tailored precisely to your room's physical dimensions.

Box-in-a-box construction isolates the cinema's interior walls, floor, and ceiling from the building's concrete structure using resilient mechanical decoupling channels, Green Glue viscoelastic damping, and whisper HVAC baffles. This achieves an STC 65+ sound isolation rating so high-volume playback never disturbs bedrooms or living areas.

A standard private cinema installation requires 4 to 8 weeks depending on millwork complexity and custom starlight ceiling weaving. Multi-seat Dirac Live acoustic phase alignment and ISF Level-III laser spectrophotometry calibration take place during the final 3 days before handover.

Yes. All AVÉRA cinema architectures feature certified Crestron, Control4, Lutron, and Savant drivers, enabling one-touch movie scenes that automatically dim constellation ceilings, adjust motorized recliners, lower anamorphic masking, and initiate playback seamlessly.
